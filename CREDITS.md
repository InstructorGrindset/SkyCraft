# Credits

## Author

**chasmlol** made SkyCraft: the idea, the Skyrim plugin and the Minecraft mod, up to 0.1.2
([chasmlol/SkyCraft](https://github.com/chasmlol/SkyCraft)).

The features this fork adds on top of 0.1.2 (looting, merchants, dungeon chests, time and sleep, deep
creatures, gear sets, portals and worlds, companions, Skooma, ...) are listed in
[docs/FEATURES.md](docs/FEATURES.md).

## Contributors

- **Higor Machado** ([waTools-br](https://github.com/waTools-br)): playtesting, and the loot fixes from
  [waTools-br/SkyCraft](https://github.com/waTools-br/SkyCraft/tree/dev-higor): loot and gear drop state
  that was never cleared (memory kept growing), drops lifted out of blocks and onto Skyrim's ground,
  and coin and odds-and-ends drops for bandits, guards, mages and other people.

Contributors are also listed in the Minecraft mod's `fabric.mod.json`, and each test build's release
notes thank the people whose commits are in it. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) says how to
contribute and how you'll be credited.

## Third-party software

SkyCraft is built on CommonLibSSE-NG, SKSE, the Address Library for SKSE Plugins, Fabric, Fabric API,
Prism Launcher and e4mc, among others. Their licenses are in
[THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).
